## 1. What Florie Beauty Is

Florie Beauty is a **premium, fun, fruit-powered skincare and makeup brand**, sold directly to customers through our own website and mobile app. We are not a marketplace like Nykaa or Amazon — we sell **only our own products**, which means every product on the site is ours, and we control quality, pricing, and the entire customer experience from browsing to delivery to after-sales support.

We're launching small and focused: **24 products total (12 skincare + 12 makeup)**, each one built around a fruit ingredient and a specific skin concern (for example, Orange for dullness, Green Tea for oily skin). The idea is to be the kind of brand a customer trusts enough to keep coming back to — helped along by a strong rewards program that gives real reasons to make a second, third, and tenth purchase.

**Why this matters for sales/marketing:** every product has a built-in story (the fruit + the skin concern it solves), which gives us a natural, ready-made angle for content, campaigns, and influencer/affiliate pitches without needing to invent a narrative from scratch.

## 2. Business Model & Launch Strategy

- **We own our stock.** Products are manufactured/sourced under our own label and held in our own warehouse — we're not drop-shipping or reselling other brands.
- **Small, curated launch.** 24 products at launch, not hundreds. This keeps quality control, marketing messaging, and inventory simple to manage while we build trust.
- **No outside sellers.** At least for now, only Florie Beauty products are sold on the platform — there's no plan to open this up to third-party vendors in the near term. This keeps the story clean: everything on the site is genuinely ours.
- **Two sales channels, one brand:** a website and a mobile app, both showing the exact same catalog, prices, and account — a customer can start browsing on their phone and finish checkout on their laptop with the same cart.
- **One command center behind the scenes.** All internal teams — the people managing products, stock, orders, finance, HR, customer support, marketing, and content — work out of a single internal system (the "Super Admin Panel," described in Section 10), rather than a patchwork of separate tools.

## 4. Brand Identity & Story

**Positioning:** premium but approachable — "soft luxury," not intimidating luxury. Cute, fruit-inspired, trustworthy, youthful, clean, modern, and built to look good on Instagram.

**The core creative device:** every product is tied to a specific fruit, and every fruit is tied to a specific skin concern. This isn't decoration — it's the entire content and merchandising engine:

| Fruit | Skin Concern It Solves | Brand Feeling |
|---|---|---|
| Orange | Dullness, pigmentation, uneven tone | Brightening, fresh glow |
| Strawberry | Acne marks, dull skin, gentle exfoliation | Cute, fresh, youthful |
| Mango | Dryness, uneven texture, dull glow | Nourishing, juicy, soft skin |
| Blueberry | Antioxidant care, tired/pollution-damaged skin | Anti-pollution, fresh |
| Green Tea | Oily skin, acne-prone, sensitive skin | Calming, oil control |
| Pomegranate | Ageing, firmness, glow, repair | Premium, anti-ageing, luxury glow |

**Product naming follows a consistent pattern** built from this system — e.g. *Orange Glow Shield Sunscreen*, *Strawberry Dew Cleanser*, *Mango Melt Moisturizer*, *Pomegranate Plump Serum*. This gives marketing a repeatable formula for naming every future product too.

**One important brand rule:** while we're inspired by how brands like Nykaa built trust in the Indian beauty market, Florie Beauty must never copy anyone's name, logo, visuals, or content — everything here is original, built under our own identity.

## 6. Shopping Experience — Skincare vs Makeup Toggle

Florie Beauty is one brand with two very different shopping moods: **Skincare** (routine-driven, ingredient/concern-focused) and **Makeup** (shade/finish/look-focused). Rather than mixing these together on one page, the site and app will have a **toggle at the top** — customers switch between "Skincare mode" and "Makeup mode" and the whole browsing experience adjusts:

- In **Skincare mode**, customers filter by skin type, concern, and SPF.
- In **Makeup mode**, customers filter by shade, finish, and look.
- Everything else — the account, cart, order history, wallet, points, membership tier — stays exactly the same no matter which mode they're browsing in. A customer can have a skincare item and a makeup item in the same cart and check out together.
- Cream Blush (and any future hybrid product) shows up in **both** modes, since it genuinely belongs to both worlds.
- The site remembers which mode a customer last used, so it opens back up the way they left it.

**Why this matters for marketing:** campaigns, banners, and blog content can now be targeted specifically to "Skincare shoppers" or "Makeup shoppers" rather than everyone at once — this is a real segmentation tool for more relevant messaging.

## 7. How the Business Runs — The Three Parts

Think of the whole system as three pieces working together:

1. **Customer Website** — for people browsing/shopping on a laptop or desktop, built with strong SEO so people find us through search engines too.
2. **Customer Mobile App** — the same shopping experience, built for phones (iOS and Android), with app-only extras like push notifications and easy sharing (e.g. referral links via WhatsApp).
3. **Super Admin Panel** — one single internal system where our own team manages everything behind the scenes: products, stock, orders, finance, people, customer support, marketing, and content. Section 10 covers this in full.

All three pieces talk to one shared backend system, so information is always consistent — a product added in the Super Admin Panel shows up correctly on both the website and the app at the same time, with no manual copying of data between systems.

## 8. Customer Website & App Experience

From a customer's point of view, the journey looks like this:

**Getting started:** a short, fun intro to the brand → sign up with just a mobile number and OTP (no password to remember) → tell us your skin type, skin concerns, and makeup preferences so we can personalize things from day one.

**Browsing:** home page with a hero banner, category browsing, and browsing by fruit or by skin concern → search → filter and sort → detailed product pages with ingredients, benefits, how-to-use instructions, and real customer reviews.

**Buying:** wishlist → cart (which follows you between devices) → apply a coupon → add an address → pay via UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets, EMI, or Cash on Delivery → get an order confirmation and tax invoice.

**After the order:** track the delivery in real time → request a return or refund if needed (with photo/video upload) → leave a review.

**Ongoing relationship:** beauty tips and blog content → the skin quiz and personalized routine → notifications → in-app customer support and live chat → account settings, including all the loyalty/rewards screens covered in Section 9.

## 9. Loyalty & Rewards Program

This is one of the biggest differentiators for repeat business, and it's worth marketing teams knowing in full detail — modeled on the kind of loyalty mechanics that keep customers coming back to category leaders like Nykaa Prive, but entirely rebuilt as our own program.

### Wallet
Every customer has one Florie Beauty Wallet balance. It gets credited automatically — from refunds, referral payouts, bonuses, and cashback-style offers — and can be used at checkout, partially or in full, combined with a normal online payment. **Important:** customers can never put their own money into the wallet — it only ever receives credits we give them. This keeps it simple, safe, and fully within regulatory boundaries.

### Bonus
- **Sign-up bonus** — the moment someone registers.
- **First-order bonus** — after their first order is delivered.
- **Birthday bonus** — every year, in their birthday month, bigger for higher-tier members.
- **Campaign bonuses** — configurable for sales and launches (e.g. extra wallet credit during a "Fruit Glow Launch Sale").

### Referral Program
Every customer gets their own shareable referral code/link (share via WhatsApp, SMS, or social media). The person they refer gets a welcome discount on their first order; the referrer earns wallet credit or points once that order is delivered. Built-in fraud checks stop the same person from gaming the system with multiple fake accounts.

### Reward Points
Customers earn 1 point per ₹1 spent, redeemable at checkout up to a set cap. Points multiply during a customer's birthday month, with higher multipliers for higher membership tiers. We can also run limited-time "double points" campaigns.

### Offers
A dedicated Offers/Deals section on the home page, offers targeted by category, product, payment method, or membership tier, plus the system automatically applying the best available discount at checkout so customers never have to hunt for the best deal themselves.

### Membership Tiers — Member / Gold / Platinum

No membership fee — customers are automatically enrolled and upgraded based on how much they spend with us over a rolling 12 months.

| Tier | Spend Needed (per year) | What They Get |
|---|---|---|
| **Member** | ₹1,500+ | Standard reward points, 1.5x birthday bonus |
| **Gold** | ₹4,000+ | Everything in Member, plus free shipping, a birthday gift, early sale access, exclusive offers, 2x birthday bonus |
| **Platinum** | ₹8,000+ | Everything in Gold, plus priority customer support, exclusive product drops, access to special point-multiplier events, 3x birthday bonus (premium gift) |

This tier system is a strong marketing hook on its own — "spend ₹4,000 a year, unlock Gold forever (as long as you keep spending)" is an easy, motivating message.

## 10. The Super Admin Panel — Our Internal Command Center

Everything our internal team needs to run the business lives in **one single internal system** — the Super Admin Panel. Different staff members see different sections based on their role, but it's all one connected system, not separate disconnected tools. Here's what it covers:

- **Catalog & Products** — add and manage every product, its photos/videos, pricing, variants and shades, and all the legal/compliance information cosmetics products are required to carry (batch numbers, expiry dates, manufacturer details, etc.). A product can't go live on the website/app until all the required legal information is filled in — this is enforced automatically, not left to memory.
- **Stock & Warehouse** — track exactly how much of everything we have, where it's stored, what's about to expire, what's low and needs reordering, and manage the full journey of stock from receiving it, storing it, picking it for an order, packing it, and handing it to our delivery partner.
- **Orders** — see every order, its status (placed → confirmed → packed → shipped → delivered), handle cancellations, returns, and refunds.
- **Finance & Tax** — tax invoices, GST reporting, profit & loss, and reconciling payments and delivery-partner remittances.
- **People (HR)** — our own employee records, attendance, leave, payroll, and hiring — entirely self-contained, doesn't touch customer-facing data at all.
- **Oversight (Auditor view)** — a read-only view for spotting unusual activity, reviewing who changed what and when — this exists purely for accountability and security, no one uses it to make day-to-day changes.
- **Customer Support** — every customer ticket, live chat conversation, and complaint, linked back to the relevant order so agents have full context.
- **Marketing** — banners, campaigns, coupons, push/email/SMS/WhatsApp messages, referral and loyalty program settings, and performance dashboards for all of it.
- **Content** — the blog, beauty tips, ingredient education, fruit-story pages, FAQs, and all legal pages (privacy policy, terms, etc.).
- **Top-Level Oversight (Super Admin)** — the very top layer, which can see a rollup of everything happening across the whole business (sales, stock alerts, complaints, campaign performance, all in one dashboard) and controls who on the team has access to what.

**Why "one panel" matters for the business:** a new hire doesn't need ten different logins — they get one login to the Super Admin Panel, and simply see the sections relevant to their job. It also means information never has to be manually copied between disconnected tools — when Warehouse marks something as shipped, Orders and Finance see it instantly, automatically.

## 12. Trust, Safety & Legal Basics

In plain terms, here's what we're required to get right, and why it matters commercially (a compliance slip in beauty ecommerce is a brand-trust problem, not just a legal one):

- **Cosmetics labeling rules (India):** every product must carry accurate batch numbers, expiry dates, manufacturer details, ingredient lists, and any test results backing up claims we make (this matters a lot for SPF claims specifically — we can't say "SPF 50" without the testing to back it up).
- **Tax invoicing:** every order gets a proper GST-compliant invoice, automatically.
- **Wallet safety:** because our wallet only ever gives credit (customers can never load their own money into it), we stay outside a whole category of banking regulation that would otherwise apply — this is a deliberate design choice, not an oversight.
- **Standard legal pages:** Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Shipping Policy, Return/Refund Policy, Cookie Policy, and clear allergy/usage disclaimers, all easily accessible to customers.
- **Customer data rights:** customers should be able to request a copy of their data or ask us to delete it — this is a newer requirement under Indian data protection law that we're building support for (it wasn't in our original plan, and has since been added).

## 13. Reports & Insights Available

The Super Admin Panel will give the business ongoing visibility into:

- **Sales:** daily/monthly totals, by product, by category, by fruit story, by skin concern, by makeup category, by city, by payment method, by coupon used.
- **Inventory:** what's in stock, what's low, what's about to expire, what's already expired or damaged.
- **Finance:** total revenue, refunds, COD vs prepaid split, margins, tax summaries.
- **Customers:** new vs repeat customers, inactive customers, high-value customers, most-searched and most-wishlisted products, cart abandonment.
- **Marketing:** campaign performance, coupon usage, push/email/SMS/WhatsApp performance, referral conversions, influencer and affiliate sales.

This is the data marketing and sales teams will use to decide what to promote, who to target, and where campaigns are or aren't working.

## 15. Current Status & What's Still Being Worked Out

A few things are worth knowing about where things currently stand:

- **The Skincare/Makeup toggle (Section 6) is a new addition** on top of the original plan — it's been fully thought through and is ready to be built, but it touches almost every part of the customer experience and a few sections of the Super Admin Panel, so it adds real scope.
- **A few things were named as "things we'll sell" (combo kits, gift sets, routine kits) but don't yet have a proper system built for them** — right now the plan only handles single products cleanly. This needs to be addressed before we can actually sell a bundled kit.
- **Customer data-rights requests** (letting a customer download or delete their data) weren't originally planned for and are being added now, to stay current with Indian data protection law.
- **A way to let customers manage their own notification preferences** (opt out of SMS but stay on email, for example) was referenced as a requirement but doesn't have a screen built for it yet — this is being added.
- **Which pincodes/areas we can actually deliver to, and where Cash on Delivery is available**, doesn't yet have a proper internal management screen — right now that information isn't easy for the team to view or update themselves.
- **Vendor/marketplace selling (letting other brands sell on our platform) is intentionally not part of the plan right now** — we're keeping this simple and single-brand for the foreseeable future.

None of these block getting started — they're just the open items worth knowing about if sales/marketing planning depends on any of them (e.g. don't promise "gift kits available at launch" until that item above is resolved).

## 16. Simple Glossary

| Term | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **SKU** | One individual product/variant we sell (e.g. "Orange Vitamin C Serum, 30ml" is one SKU) |
| **PLP / PDP** | The page showing a list of products / the page showing details of one specific product |
| **COD** | Cash on Delivery — paying in cash when the order arrives |
| **GST** | Goods & Services Tax — India's standard sales tax |
| **RTO** | "Return to Origin" — when a delivery couldn't be completed and the package comes back to our warehouse |
| **Wallet** | The customer's Florie Beauty credit balance — never loaded with their own money, only ever credited by us |
| **Membership Tier** | Member / Gold / Platinum — status level based on how much a customer has spent with us in the last year |
| **Segment** | Either "Skincare" or "Makeup" — the two halves of our catalog the toggle switches between |
| **Super Admin Panel** | The single internal system our own team uses to run every part of the business |
