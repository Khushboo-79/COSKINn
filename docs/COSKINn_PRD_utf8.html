1. Purpose & Vision

COSKINn is a premium, Gen-Z-friendly, fruit-powered skincare and makeup ecommerce brand, launching as an inventory-led / private-label business with a limited, curated product range (12 skincare + 12 makeup SKUs at launch).

This PRD defines what must be built for the first production launch: a customer website (React + Tailwind CSS), a customer mobile app (React Native), and a Node.js/PostgreSQL backend on AWS ΓÇö including a full customer loyalty and monetary ecosystem (Wallet, Bonus, Referral, Reward Points, Offers, and a tiered Membership program) alongside the operational back-office needed to run the business.

1.1 Problem Statement

Indian beauty ecommerce is dominated by large multi-brand marketplaces. There is room for a tightly curated, ingredient-story-driven (fruit + skin concern), soft-luxury brand that gives customers a simple, trustworthy, habit-forming shopping experience ΓÇö with the kind of loyalty mechanics (points, wallet, tiers) that keep shoppers of category leaders like Nykaa coming back.

1.2 Product Vision

Become the go-to destination for fruit-based, concern-driven skincare and SPF-safe makeup for value-conscious Gen-Z/young-millennial shoppers in India, with a loyalty program compelling enough to drive repeat purchase from order one.

3. Scope

3.1 In Scope (v1 / MVP)

  • Customer Website (React + Tailwind CSS) and Customer App (React Native) ΓÇö full storefront
  • Admin, Product, Inventory, Order, Warehouse, Customer Support, Marketing panels
  • Razorpay payment integration (UPI/cards/netbanking/wallets/EMI) + COD
  • ShadowFox shipping/delivery integration
  • Wallet, Bonus, Referral, Reward Points, Offers, and tiered Membership (Member/Gold/Platinum)
  • Skin quiz, routine builder, reviews & ratings, beauty content/blog
  • Tax invoice generation and basic finance reporting

  • Marketplace/third-party vendor onboarding and seller panel
  • HR panel, Auditor panel, advanced finance/tax automation (Phase 2)
  • Wallet top-up/add-money by customers (system-credited wallet only, to avoid PPI/RBI licensing)
  • International shipping
